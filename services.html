What I Do

You don't need a 50-slide PowerPoint deck about data strategy. You need someone to fix the specific, thorny problem that is costing you money right now. Here is how I can help:

Find the Hidden Costs

The Problem: Your ERP says you made a 25% margin. Your sales reps are hitting their KPIs. But your bank account is shrinking, and you don't know why.

What I'll Do: I'll find where the money is going, whether it's tied up in dead inventory, hidden carrying costs, or misaligned KPIs. Then I'll build the strategy to recover that capital and fix the process that caused it.

Fix Your Broken Data

The Problem: Your catalog: Barcodes are missing, UNSPSC codes are generic, and your Ariba punchout is failing. You have a massive catalog and no idea where the errors are.

What I'll Do: I'll run a weighted, 16-point data audit against your inventory. I'll give you a Report Card that shows exactly which fields are broken, which vendor records are incomplete, and the custom scripts to fix it.

Find the Missing Sales

The Problem: Your dashboard shows flat revenue. Your sales team says they are converting product lines. But you aren't growing, and you can't figure out why.

What I'll Do: I'll compare your Prior Year-To-Date (PYTD) to Current Year-To-Date (YTD) at the customer and vendor level. I'll expose the hidden product line swaps and the silent customer churn that standard reports miss.

Engagement Models

Every business has a different timeline and a different budget. Whether you need a deep-dive audit, an ongoing fractional engineer, or an emergency fix, here is how we can structure the work:

Build the Tool You Actually Need

The Problem: You are paying $12,000 to $15,000 a year for Salesforce or HubSpot, but your team only uses a fraction of the features. The interface is too slow, and you just want a fast, offline tool for data entry.

What I'll Do: I'll build you a custom "Gap App" using my Micro-Kernel architecture. It will run locally on your machines, load in milliseconds, and do exactly what your workflow requires - no cloud, no subscriptions.

Strategic Audit (1-Week Deep Dive)

The Problem: You know your data is a mess, but you don't know where to start or which errors are actually costing you money.

What You Get: Review existing data structures, find hidden costs, and deliver a plain-text road map. No 50-slide PowerPoint decks.

Emergency Fix (Immediate Triage)

The Problem: A pipeline just broke, an ERP update failed, or a vendor feed is stalling your warehouse routing right now.

What You Get: Fast, hands-on execution to get your systems back online and your data flowing without friction.

Where I Do My Best Work

I don't try to be everything to everyone. I focus on industries where the data represents physical things - where a Unit of Measure error costs real money and a dead SKU takes up real warehouse space.

Distribution & Supply Chain

I understand the physical reality of your inventory. From raw ERP data to procurement platforms, I standardize the mapping, trace the transactions, and identify the dead stock that is driving up your carrying costs.

B2B Manufacturing

I know how the factory floor connects to the General Ledger. I handle Bill of Materials (BOM) data, SDS compliance, Prop 65 regulations, and identify the structural risks of high vendor concentration.

Independent Sales & Agencies

I build high-volume local CRMs and pipeline trackers for reps who don't need the cloud. If you need fast, offline contact management without a five-figure annual SaaS subscription, I build the tools.
